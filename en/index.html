About Kyrion

An integrated scientific ecosystem

Kyrion Scientific was born from the integration of scientific knowledge, strategy and market vision to develop solutions, enable technologies and create new businesses in health and biotechnology.

Kyrion Scientific brings together specialised, complementary business units, uniting scientific knowledge, strategy and innovation to develop solutions, structure businesses and drive the growth of healthcare companies.

  • Applied science

    Scientific knowledge oriented towards solving real problems.

  • Strategy

    We turn scientific knowledge and market vision into growth strategies for health and biotech companies. We structure health and diagnostic services, develop commercial partnerships and expand business opportunities, focusing on competitiveness and profitability.

  • Innovation

    Science, technology and creativity to turn ideas into solutions applicable to research and routine.
